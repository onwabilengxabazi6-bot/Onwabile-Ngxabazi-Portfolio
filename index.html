About Me

I am someone who works at the intersection of practical engineering and creative systems design, where real-world hardware meets futuristic digital experiences. My work combines two worlds that most people keep separate. On one side, I design and explore real engineering systems like DIY home theater and amplifier projects such as SHAKUMBA 2.0 built with DSP processing, modular electronics, and structured audio architecture. On the other side, I build cybersecurity-focused and AI-powered systems with futuristic interfaces, interactive dashboards, and immersive digital experiences. My passion for Technology × Wildlife Conservation inspires me to explore how AI, monitoring systems, drones, and secure digital infrastructure can help solve real-world environmental problems. I am currently focused on becoming a Cybersecurity Analyst while continuously building skills in engineering, cybersecurity, AI systems, and software development.
